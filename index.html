THUMBSHOT

Thumbshot is a quick tank duel for iPhone that you play with one thumb. Play the CPU, or a friend on the same phone.

How to play

Your barrel swings by itself. Put your thumb down to stop it and charge up the power, then lift it to fire. The windsock shows which way the wind pushes your shot. A direct hit takes a quarter of the other tank's health; empty their bar to win.

In Duo, red plays on the left half of the screen and blue on the right.
