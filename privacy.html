Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Thumbshot does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your settings (sound on or off, and your last choices for world, wind and CPU level) are saved only on your own iPhone, inside the app. They never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to view, export or delete on our side.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
